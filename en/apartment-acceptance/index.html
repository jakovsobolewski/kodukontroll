Apartment inspection before the acceptance act

Check the apartment before you accept it.

An independent inspection of a new apartment, house or completed renovation before you sign the acceptance act and make the final payment.

We check the quality of finishes, plumbing, electrics, windows, doors and building services, record the defects we find and prepare a clear list of remarks for the developer or contractor.

01Why

Why an independent acceptance inspection

Even a brand-new apartment can have defects that are hard to notice without experience and a careful check:

  • uneven walls, floors and ceilings;
  • paint and finishing defects;
  • damage to windows, doors and surfaces;
  • incorrectly installed sockets, switches and sanitary fittings;
  • leaks and inadequate sealing;
  • problems with ventilation, heating or underfloor heating;
  • cracks, chips, scratches and unfinished work;
  • deviations from the design or the agreed specification.

Once the act is signed, getting defects fixed can be harder. The best moment to inspect is after the work is finished but before the apartment is finally accepted.

02Checks

What we check

The exact scope depends on the property, the apartment’s specification and the documents provided.

Walls and ceilings

We check:

  • paint and finish quality;
  • cracks, chips, stains and other visible defects;
  • flatness of surfaces within the agreed method of checking;
  • quality of corners and junctions;
  • unfinished work and damage.

Floors and skirting

We check:

  • condition of the floor covering;
  • level differences, damage and visible unevenness;
  • quality of joints and junctions;
  • skirting installation;
  • creaks, movement and other noticeable defects.

Windows and doors

We check:

  • opening and closing;
  • adjustment of sashes and door leaves;
  • condition of frames, glazing and hardware;
  • seals and visible gaps;
  • scratches, chips and other damage;
  • operation of locks and handles.

Bathroom and plumbing

We check:

  • installation of basins, baths, showers and WCs;
  • operation of mixers, drains and shut-off valves;
  • tightness of accessible connections;
  • visible leaks;
  • quality of silicone joints and sealing;
  • falls and drainage where they can be checked;
  • fixing of sanitary fittings.

Electrics and lighting

In a standard inspection we assess:

  • operation of accessible sockets and switches;
  • the installed lighting;
  • placement of electrical fittings;
  • visible installation quality;
  • basic operation of connected equipment;
  • visible defects and potentially unsafe solutions.

If special measurements or a check of the distribution board, earthing or protective devices are needed, a qualified specialist is brought in. Such a check is agreed separately.

Ventilation and heating

We check:

  • draught and basic operation of accessible ventilation elements;
  • installation and condition of grilles and valves;
  • accessible heating elements;
  • operation of thermostats and controls;
  • visible signs of incorrect installation or unfinished work.

Measuring ventilation performance or heating system parameters is agreed separately.

Balcony, terrace and extra rooms

Where present, we check:

  • finishes and visible surfaces;
  • doors, windows and railings;
  • falls and drainage where accessible;
  • the storeroom, parking space and other elements included in the specification.

Furniture and appliances

If the apartment is handed over with a kitchen, built-in furniture or appliances, checking them can be included in the service.

We assess installation quality, fixings, gaps, front adjustment, appliance ventilation, access to services and the basic operation of the equipment.

04Process

How an apartment inspection works

  1. 1

    You send us the details

    To quote a price we need:

    • the address and floor area of the apartment;
    • the number of rooms;
    • the floor plan;
    • a description of the specification and the work done;
    • the contract, design or construction documents, if available;
    • photographs;
    • the planned handover date.
  2. 2

    We agree the scope and the price

    Before the visit we define:

    • exactly what needs to be checked;
    • which specialists are needed;
    • how long the inspection will take;
    • what the final report will contain;
    • the final price.

    The inspection goes ahead only once you have confirmed the quote.

  3. 3

    We inspect the apartment

    The specialist walks through the apartment, checks the accessible elements and records the defects found.

    If you wish, a representative of the developer or contractor can be present.

  4. 4

    We deliver the report

    You receive a structured list of remarks with photographs and recommendations. The standard report is usually ready within 2–3 working days.

    If needed, you can order a re-inspection once the defects have been fixed.

06Limits

Good to know

A standard inspection is visual and non-destructive: nothing is opened up and no equipment is dismantled.

Concealed services and structures are checked only as far as they are accessible, on the basis of the documents provided, or with separately agreed diagnostic methods.

A standard Kodukontroll report is an independent record of the inspection. It does not replace a court-ordered expert opinion, an official measurement protocol or the opinion of a certified specialist where such a document is required for court proceedings or a formal dispute.

07FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, write to us. We usually reply the same working day.

When is the best time to inspect an apartment?

After construction and finishing work is complete, but before the acceptance act is signed and the final payment made.

Can I order an inspection after signing the act?

Yes. We can inspect an apartment you have already accepted and help record the defects found. Before the act is signed, however, it is usually easier to get them fixed.

Does the developer’s representative have to be present?

No, but it can be useful: the specialist can point out the defects and explain the remarks on the spot.

How long does the inspection take?

Usually 1.5 to 3 hours. It depends on the floor area, the number of rooms and the scope of the inspection.

Will you definitely find defects?

No. The purpose of the inspection is an objective assessment, not remarks at any cost. If the apartment has been finished well, the report says so.

Can you check that the defects have been fixed?

Yes. Once the corrections are done, you can order a re-inspection.

Do you check concealed work?

Without opening structures, concealed work can only be assessed from accessible elements, photographs, acts and the documents provided. Such work is best checked before it is covered up.

Is the report an official expert opinion?

The standard report is an independent record of the inspection carried out. If you need an official protocol or an opinion from a specialist with a particular qualification, this must be agreed before the inspection.

01Why

The installer grades their own work. That is not enough.

Bespoke furniture often costs more than a car. Yet it is signed off with a quick look and a handshake, without anyone measuring, testing or writing anything down.

I

Defects show up later

A sagging door, a loose wall fixing or an unsealed joint is invisible on day one. Six months later, a warranty dispute is much harder, because there is no evidence.

II

You don't have to be the expert

You shouldn't need to know the correct hinge reveal or how a stone worktop must be supported. We do, and we tell you plainly what is right and what is not.

III

The final payment is your only leverage

Before the last invoice is paid, the installer has every reason to fix things fast. After it, the problems are yours. The inspection happens at exactly the right moment.

02Checks

Eight areas, over a hundred checkpoints.

We don't "have a look". We measure, open, close, load and compare against the drawings. Every point is graded: OK, cosmetic, significant or critical.

  1. 01

    Level and plumb

    Carcasses, fronts and worktops level, plumb and in line. Measured, not eyeballed.

  2. 02

    Gaps and alignment

    Even door and drawer reveals, flush fronts, symmetry and handle alignment.

  3. 03

    Doors, drawers, mechanisms

    Every door and drawer opened and closed. Hinges, runners, soft-close, lift and corner mechanisms.

  4. 04

    Surfaces and edges

    Scratches, chips, veneer and edge-banding quality, colour consistency, finish defects.

  5. 05

    Fixings and load

    Wall fixings, anchors and substrate, wall-cabinet load, worktop and shelf support.

  6. 06

    Appliances and services

    Integrated appliances, ventilation, lighting, water connections and seals, electrical positions.

  7. 07

    Conformity to order

    Dimensions, materials, hardware and solutions match the drawings and the quote you are paying for.

  8. 08

    Documentation

    Warranty terms, care instructions, appliance documents and a proper handover certificate.

03Process

Four steps from booking to a confirmed result.

  1. 1

    Booking

    Send us the drawings and the quote. We agree a time, ideally on the last day of installation or right after.

    Same week
  2. 2

    On-site inspection

    The expert goes through the whole installation point by point, measuring and photographing. With you and the installer present if you wish.

    2–3 hours
  3. 3

    Report

    Every defect with a photo, location, severity and a recommended fix. Ready to forward to the installer.

    Within 48 h
  4. 4

    Re-inspection

    Once the installer has fixed the defects, we check them and confirm in writing that the work is complete.

    Optional
